In 2009, mathematician Simon Blackburn formulated a precise model for what he called a perfect
parking maneuver. First, there is no handbrake skidding and no multi-point shuffling. You
reverse into the space at full steering lock in a single continuous arc, stopping the exact moment
your car is parallel to the curb. Then, you straighten your front wheels and drive straight
forward into the middle of the space. To find the minimum space required,
we only need to analyze the geometry of that reversing sweep.
