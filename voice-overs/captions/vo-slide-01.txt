You are driving down a busy street and you finally spot a gap between two parked cars.
The road is wide so you have plenty of room to swing out, but the space itself looks uncomfortably
tight. Nobody enjoys shuffling back and forth, inching closer to the curb a few centimeters at
a time. You want to reverse into the space in one smooth sweep and pull straight forward to park.
So here is the mathematical question. How much extra length,
beyond the length of your own car, does that parking space actually need?
