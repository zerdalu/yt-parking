To turn a real car into clean geometry, imagine slicing the vehicle horizontally
right through the centers of its four wheels, looking straight down from above.
The extreme outer body forms a simple rectangle, labeled A, B, C, D.
The curb runs along the straight line through A, E, F, and D. Or, if you prefer never to scrape
your alloy wheels on concrete, an imaginary line a few centimeters to the right of the curb.
Notice that our curbside wheels, centered at points E and F,
line up closely with the left edge A to D of the car.
